My Hero - Rod Wave 🎤

Rod Wave is one of my favourite artists in the music industry. I would call him my 'hero' because when I was a teenager his music helped me find confidence. I was very athletic and before sports games or trainings I would listen to his music to hype me up.

Who is Rod Wave?
Rod Wave is an American rapper, singer and songwriter.
Music Genre
His music is a mixture of R&B, hip hop and rap. I could spend hours listening to his music because I personally find it relaxing and relateable.
Fun Fact
Rod Wave's real name is Rodarius Marcell Green and now goes by Rod Wave as it reflects his name and music style.
