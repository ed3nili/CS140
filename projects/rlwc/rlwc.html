What is the Rugby League World Cup?

The Rugby League World cup is held every 4 years. Professional athletes compete playing the Rugby League representing their countries, nations and family. The RLWC isn't just about sport but it is about unity and leagacy. This table shows the standings and points of each team after the round/pool games were played.
