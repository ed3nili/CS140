My title and topic: Food around the Pacific

I chose this topic because I think it will be fun, informative and a creative way to share about FOOD specifically in the Pacific. Since attending school I have missed my dad's homecooked island food, but I've also been able to experience food from places around the world. I will be able to showcase popular dishes from 4 different islands in the Pacific.

CONTENT

  1. Home page will include an introduction into the Pacific. It will showcase the geographical mapping aswell as introducing the 4 different Islands. The homepage will also explain how each page will be a specific island with a receipe of one of the famous/well known dishes.
  2. Page 1 will be SAMOA. The food that will be displayed will be KOKO ALAISA (Samoan Cocoa Rice). I will explain it's ingredients and how it's made.
  3. Page 2 will be FIJI. The food that will be displayed will be KOKODA.
  4. Page 3 will be AOTEAROA/New Zealand. The food displayed will be Hangi.
  5. Page 4 will TONGA. Featuring Lu Pulu & Lu Sipi.
  6. Page 5 will be a summary and comparison between all the pacific foods. I also want to show the comparisons through a table.

FORM

I plan to use a form to see which food the guests enjoy and prefer. It will ask for their information then their preference and WHY they choose that specific food over the others.
