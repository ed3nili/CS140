questions/engagement on canvas

  1. JPEG are good for pictures of people, places/landscapes, food a.k.a real life pictures. (Lossy)
  2. GIF is good for short animations, simple graphics or images with few colors. (Lossy)
  3. PNG is unique because it keeps the quality when saved. It supports transparent backgrounds so it's useful for icons, buttons, logos. (Lossless)
